Inconsistency in Trino Statistics Regarding Last Task End Time
I recently noticed an inconsistency in the reporting of `lastTaskEndTime` in https://github.com/trinodb/trino/blob/master/core/trino-main/src/main/java/io/trino/event/QueryMonitor.java#L633. It appears that the lastTaskEndTime is actually the time it took for the last task running in a leaf stage to end i.e. it is actually `lastLeafTaskEndTime`. Shouldn't the `lastTaskEndTime` be the end time of the last running task on the Trino engine, be it leaf or non-leaf?
