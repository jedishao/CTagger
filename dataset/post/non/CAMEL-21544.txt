camel-http-common - Add option to turn on|off whether application/x-www-form-urlencoded should populate Map body with form
Only headers is populated with the form data, and the body is left as-is.

However in CEQ/vertx/camel-main then we use platform-http-vertx that populates both headers and body by default.

To align CSB and CEQ we would need to do the same.

So we need an option to turn this on|off. We can maybe turn this on so they are the same out of the box. And users that rely on the old behaviour can turn it off.
