### Minimal reproduce step

start a standalone Pulsar with `managedLedgerMaxEntriesPerLedger=10000 managedLedgerMinLedgerRolloverTimeMinutes=0`
```
managedLedgerMaxEntriesPerLedger=10000 managedLedgerMinLedgerRolloverTimeMinutes=0 docker/pulsar/scripts/apply-config-from-env.py conf/standalone.conf
PULSAR_STANDALONE_USE_ZOOKEEPER=1 bin/pulsar standalone -nss -nfw 2>&1 | tee standalone.log
```

Create a topic with 10 partitions. Produce & consume messages with pulsar-perf. It might require `--message-key-generation-mode autoIncrement` as a parameter to `pulsar-perf` and also disabling batching with `--disable-batching` so that all partitions rollover at the same time.
