Deprecate Netty3 in Druid
### Description

Druid still depends on Netty3 which has vulnerabilities, such as [CVE-2023-44487](https://nvd.nist.gov/vuln/detail/CVE-2023-44487). Certain downstream applications such as Hive are unable to completely remove the netty3 dependencies due to being dependent on druid. Please ref [HIVE-25013](https://issues.apache.org/jira/browse/HIVE-25013). Druid should remove its dependency on Netty3.
