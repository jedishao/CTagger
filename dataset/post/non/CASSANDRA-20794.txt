Generate autocompletion scripts for the nodetool command during the build
All picocli-based applications can have command line completion in Bash or ZSH Unix shells. Picocli can generate an autocompletion script tailored to your application.

It may be useful to generate your completion scripts automatically during the build.
See the details below:
