Developing a Custom ReplicationEndpoint to Support External Storage Integration
*Develop a Custom Replication Endpoint*
Implement a custom replication endpoint to support the backup of WALs to external storage systems, such as HDFS-compliant storages (including HDFS, S3, ADLS, and GCS via respective Hadoop connectors).

*Support for Bulk-loaded Files*
Add functionality to back up bulk-loaded files in addition to regular WALs.

*Ensure Process Durability*
Ensure the backup process is durable, with no WALs being missed, even in the event of issues in the cluster.
