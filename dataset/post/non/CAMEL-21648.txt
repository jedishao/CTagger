camel-opentelemetry: Provide a way to customize Span creation
Sometimes folks have specific tracing requirements that are not easy to fulfil out of the box with the OpenTelemetry component.

So, it would be good to have a way to hook into the span creation process and have finer control over it. E.g via some kind of 'customizer' that provides access to the SpanBuilder, so that customizations could be applied.
