Update Docs page for 3rd Party Plugins 
The Plugin doc page under [integrating/plugins|https://github.com/apache/cassandra/tree/trunk/doc/modules/cassandra/pages/integrating/plugins/index.adoc] has only two plugins, but of which are obsolete as of Cassandra 5.0.  This suggests the entire page should be deleted to avoid confusing users.
 # The Stratio's [Lucene|https://github.com/instaclustr/cassandra-lucene-index] plugin was maintained by Instaclustr (NetApp) but will be retired with Cassandra 5.0 in favor of SAI indexing. It should be removed.
 # [CAPI-Rowcache|https://github.com/ppc64le/capi-rowcache] for IBM Power8 system with a CAPI flash card hasn't been maintained or updated for the past 7 years with the last release in June 2017. I've attempted to contact the author by email, but have received no response.  In addition the Power8 has reached end-of-service-life in October 2024.
