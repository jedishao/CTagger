Move Bylaws from documentation to community section and drop obsolete content
The community section in the website already has a Bylaws section but the latter points to the wiki that is now deprecated. After the wiki migration (HIVE-28666) the Bylaws content is under the Documentation section in the website.

The goal of this ticket is to move the Bylaws content to the appropriate place under the community section and align/remove/update links pointing to this section.

There are also four pages holding old proposals to change the bylaws that were incorporated in the main content some time ago and they are now obsolete and redundant. The plan is to remove these obsolete pages as part of this change.
