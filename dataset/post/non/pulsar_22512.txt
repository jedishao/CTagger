### Motivation

When a broker or proxy is deployed behind a reverse proxy, the origin IP of requests will always be IP of the proxy, Therefore the XFF(X-Forwarded-For) request header is often used to preserve the real client IPs, its a de-facto standard. However, the broker and proxy do not currently handle this.

### Solution

Configure [ForwardedRequestCustomizer](https://eclipse.dev/jetty/javadoc/jetty-12/org/eclipse/jetty/server/ForwardedRequestCustomizer.html) for jetty [httpConfiguration](https://github.com/apache/pulsar/blob/20915d1c438783c05778d98a3b77ec485b79d79d/pulsar-broker/src/main/java/org/apache/pulsar/broker/web/WebService.java#L105) to handle these headers and support this feature.
