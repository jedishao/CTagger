Reading mmapped trie-index exceeding 2GiB results in IndexOutOfBoundsException
{color:#1f2328}Memory-mapping is done in buffers of size less than 2GiB.{color}
{color:#1f2328}When these buffers aren't aligned to 4KiB and the trie-index file {color}{color:#1f2328}spans many buffers then reading it results in going out of buffer bounds.{color}

To fix it we need to {color:#1f2328}make sure that the buffers are correctly aligned.{color}
