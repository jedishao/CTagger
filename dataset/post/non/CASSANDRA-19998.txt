*Current Mitigation:*

1. Disable zone reclaim mode:


{code:java}
echo 0 > /proc/sys/vm/zone_reclaim_mode{code}
 

 

2. Disable transparent hugepage defragmentation:
   
{code:java}
   echo never > /sys/kernel/mm/transparent_hugepage/defrag{code}
 

 

3. If the scrub doesn't resolve the issue, perform the following steps:
   - Stop the node.
   - Remove the `{*}*Summary.db`{*} files for the affected table.
   - Restart the node.
