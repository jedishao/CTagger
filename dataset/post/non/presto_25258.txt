Enhance PrestoToVelox Plan conversion for plan checker mode
TableWriteNodes were skipped in plan checker in native side-car because they have some fields that are populated by the scheduler. https://github.com/prestodb/presto/pull/25115

This is restrictive. An alternate solution could be to enhance PrestoToVeloxPlanConversion to add an option for planChecker mode and skip the scheduler populated fields in TableWrite. This mode could be used in the future for other restrictions.
