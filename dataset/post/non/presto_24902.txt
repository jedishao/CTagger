Optimize array_agg/set_agg order by to use array_sort
Lot of times users do:

ARRAY_AGG(v order by k)

but that will eliminate partial agg and also expensive. instead one can do (assuming v is orderable)

TRANSFORM(ARRAY_SORT(ARRAY_AGG(ROW(k, v)), x->x[2])

We can do this in the operator as well for performance.
