Change RepairTokenRangeSplitter's partitions_per_assignment to not be based on deprecated repair_session_max_tree_depth
As {{repair_session_max_tree_depth}} has been deprecated in favor of {{repair_session_space}}, rather than using {{2^repair_session_max_tree_depth}} for the default max partition count per assignment, we should consider just setting it to 1,048,576 (2^20, what repair_session_max_tree_depth previously defaulted to) or something else.
