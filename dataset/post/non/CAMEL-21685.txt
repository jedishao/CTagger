camel-jbang - Deprecate modeline and WARN if in use
The modeline was an experiment from camel-k for cramming everyting into a single file, and instruct the kamel cli to append args (modeline) when you type kamel run.

We should deprecate modeline we have in camel-yaml-dsl / camel-dsl-modeline and make Camel do a WARN log when starting if modeline are in use.

We do the same for the deprecated kebab-case in YAML DSL.
