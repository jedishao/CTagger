Merge adjacent union distinct
Current Hive compiles
"SELECT * FROM TBL1 UNION SELECT * FROM TBL2 UNION SELECT * FROM TBL3"
to
{code:java}
TS - GBY - RS
TS - GBY - RS - GBY - RS
           TS - GBY - RS - GBY {code}
This can be optimized as follows:
{code:java}
TS - GBY - RS
TS - GBY - RS
TS - GBY - RS - GBY {code}
