In a multi-master multi-slave cluster setup, when a master node goes down, why does the client attempt to connect to the original master node?

The Redisson client is configured with the readMode set to SLAVE.

The configured cache employs a multi-master multi-slave setup, where each shard corresponds to one master and three slaves.

In my scenario, I shut down one master and one slave within a shard. Consequently, one of the remaining two slaves successfully switches to become the new master. At this point, the cluster's configuration for this shard becomes one master and one slave.

However, my Redisson client reports that it cannot connect to the original master node. This issue has been perplexing me because, as per my understanding, the cluster is still fully operational, with both master and slave nodes available.
