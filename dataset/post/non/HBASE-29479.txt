QuotaCache is not populated until first run of QuotaRefresherChore
HBASE-29387 reduced the default interval between runs of QuotaRefresherChore. This exposed an issue for RegionServers upon started. Their quota caches contain incorrect information until QuotaRefresherChore, which by default is not for 12 hours.

The logic of QuotaRefresherChore should be run immediately upon RegionServer startup, and then every 12 hours after that.
