Saxon xslt concat does not work correctly on whitespaces
There is a bug in saxon HE starting from saxon HE 10. The xslt concat function with multi parameters:  concat(s,s,...) does not correctly concat consecutive whitespaces.

concat('x', ' ', ' ', 'y') gives 'x y', it removes one of the whitespaces.

This has been documented in https://saxonica.plan.io/issues/6494
There is no release in Saxon HE yet, but once it's available a dependency upgrade should be done.
