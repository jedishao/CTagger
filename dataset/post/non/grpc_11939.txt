Hi grpc team,

Netty-handler v 4.110.Final is susceptible to CVE-2025-24970 and included in grpc-netty-shaded. A brief look into your use makes me think it grpc is not susceptible to it, but the netty version should be updated to 4.118.Final or higher. Especially because security scanning software will flag grpc due to its presence.
