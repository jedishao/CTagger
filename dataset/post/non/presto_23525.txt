Tiny table joining two large tables should pre join both
We have seen a number of prod queries like:

select .. T1 JOIN T2 ON T1.x = T2.x JOIN T3 ON T2.y=T3.y

And T3 is tiny - a few thousand rows but T1 and T2 are several 100B rows. In that case, we should join the two large tables T1 and T2 separately with T3 and then join the result (maybe make it cost based)
