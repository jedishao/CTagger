Parallelize the archival of compacted files 
While splitting a region in hbase it has to cleanup compacted files for bookkeeping.
 
Currently we do it sequentially and that is good enough because for hdfs as it is a fast operation. When we do the same in s3 it becomes a issue. We need to paralleize this to make it faster.
