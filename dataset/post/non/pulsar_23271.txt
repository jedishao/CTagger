### Minimal reproduce step

The Key_Shared subscription contains "recently joined consumer" logic to present out-of-order delivery when new consumers join and leave in KeySharedMode.AUTO_SPLIT. This logic is unnecessary for KeySharedMode.STICKY.
The "recently joined consumer" logic is described in [PIP-282: Change definition of the recently joined consumers position](https://github.com/apache/pulsar/blob/master/pip/pip-282.md).


### What did you expect to see?

The "recently joined consumer" logic shouldn't be applied when using KeySharedMode.STICKY.

### What did you see instead?

The "recently joined consumer" logic isn't currently limited to KeySharedMode.AUTO_SPLIT.
