Unify compression codec and APIs across modules
## Background

Presto use different compression codec and compressor interfaces across modules.

### presto-spi
[PageCompressor](https://github.com/prestodb/presto/blob/master/presto-spi/src/main/java/com/facebook/presto/spi/page/PageCompressor.java) is defined in presto-spi as a compressor interface, which is exactly identical to [ io.airlift.compress.Compressor](https://github.com/airlift/aircompressor/blob/0.27/src/main/java/io/airlift/compress/Compressor.java) in aircompressor.  The duplication was to reduce dependencies within presto-spi. However, in modules that depends on it,  additional adapters are required to utilize the compressor that has been implemented in aircompressor.

### presto-orc
presto-orc define its own codec in [CompressionKind](https://github.com/prestodb/presto/blob/master/presto-orc/src/main/java/com/facebook/presto/orc/metadata/CompressionKind.java), supporting NONE, ZLIB, SNAPPY, LZ4, and ZSTD.

It also introduces [OrcDecompressor](https://github.com/prestodb/presto/blob/master/presto-orc/src/main/java/com/facebook/presto/orc/OrcDecompressor.java) interface.

### presto-parquet
presto-parquet defines its own [ParquetCompressor](https://github.com/prestodb/presto/blob/master/presto-parquet/src/main/java/com/facebook/presto/parquet/writer/ParquetCompressor.java) interface.

### presto-hive
presto-hive defines its own codec in [HiveCompressionCodec](https://github.com/prestodb/presto/blob/master/presto-hive/src/main/java/com/facebook/presto/hive/HiveCompressionCodec.java), supporting NONE, SNAPPY, GZIP, LZ4, and ZSTD.

## Proposal
It would be better if we can unify compression codec interfaces across module.
Since most implementations of the interfaces defined in those modules are based on aircompressor, it would be a reasonable choice to adopt it as the base abstraction and extend it if specific implementation needed.

## Benefits
The compression codec definitions and implementations will be consistent across modules, ensuring better compression management, eliminating unnecessary adapters, and enhancing extensibility.
