Remove `org.apache.druid.discovery.BrokerClient` by switching to `org.apache.druid.sql.client.BrokerClient`
`org.apache.druid.discovery.BrokerClient` was deprecated in favor of `org.apache.druid.sql.client.BrokerClient`  in https://github.com/apache/druid/pull/17382.

Currently, there's only one usage of `org.apache.druid.discovery.BrokerClient` in `SegmentLoadStatusFetcher` in the MSQ [code](https://github.com/apache/druid/blob/master/extensions-core/multi-stage-query/src/main/java/org/apache/druid/msq/exec/SegmentLoadStatusFetcher.java#L241). We can remove that instance altogether and migrate to using the new `org.apache.druid.sql.client.BrokerClient` which is a more robust client.
