Using $bucket should preserve bucketed execution
Sometimes in addition to the bucket key, a user might want to do other things on a per bucket basis - like sorting per bucket in the middle of the query. But the moment there is anything other than the bucket key is used for partitioning, we disallow bucketed execution. For example

select .. row_number() over(partition by "$bucket" order by s1,s2) as rn from T1 join T2 using(bucket_key)

will not do bucketed join but structurally looks like its doable.
