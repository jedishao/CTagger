Support Direct I/O for SSTable writing
Based on the throughput improvements observed in CASSANDRA-18464, which introduced commit log Direct I/O support, it is logical to extend Direct I/O support to writing SSTables (specifically during memtable flushing and compaction).

The advantages of Direct I/O include consistent disk performance, as it eliminates reliance on the page cache for flushing dirty pages to disk, thus increasing write control. This is particularly beneficial during the writing of large SSTable files, such as during higher-tier compactions, as it prevents significant page cache thrashing with pages that are unlikely to be used again before being evicted. Additionally, Direct I/O reduces latency associated with double buffering, which occurs in both the application and the page cache in tradional buffered I/O.

This Jira ticket aims to initiate a discussion and gather feedback on this proposal. I am particularly interested in identifying any potential drawbacks that may affect specific workloads.

If the feature is agreed upon, extensive performance testing will be essential to evaluate its impact and determine its overall value.
