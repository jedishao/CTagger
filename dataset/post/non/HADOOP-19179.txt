ABFS: Support FNS Accounts over BlobEndpoint
As a pre-requisite to deprecating WASB Driver, ABFS Driver will need to match FNS account support as intended by WASB driver. This will provide an official migrating means for customers still using the legacy driver to ABFS Driver. 

 

Parent Jira for WASB deprecation: [HADOOP-19178] WASB Driver Deprecation and eventual removal - ASF JIRA (apache.org)
