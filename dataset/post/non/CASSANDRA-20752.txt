Flush SAI segment builder when current sstable writer is switched
With sharded compaction, sstable writer may be switched when reaching boundary in `SSTableRewriter#switchWriter`. But the switched writer's segment builders are not flushed until all sharded are written and committed. 

This unccessarily retains memory causing smaller segments and may even cause OOM due to segment builder's min-flush-size.

We can extend `SSTableFlushObserver` to be notified on sstable switched
