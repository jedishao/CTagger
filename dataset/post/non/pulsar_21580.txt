### Motivation

Now we have 8 methods to create an ack command. We should consider to check which one is redundant.
<img width="638" alt="image" src="https://github.com/apache/pulsar/assets/12592133/a833955f-e1e5-4df9-bb7e-f9cb78917239">
See more details at https://github.com/apache/pulsar/pull/21419.

### Solution

Only keep `public static ByteBuf newMultiMessageAck(long consumerId, TxnID txnID,
            List<Triple<Long, Long, ConcurrentBitSetRecyclable>> entries, long requestID) ` and mark other methods as deprecated.
