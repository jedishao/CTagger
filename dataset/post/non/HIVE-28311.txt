Backward compatibility of java.sql.Date and java.sql.Timestamp in hive-serde
HIVE-20007 introduced {{org.apache.hadoop.hive.common.type.Date}} and {{org.apache.hadoop.hive.common.type.Timestamp}} to replace {{java.sql.Date}} and {{{}java.sql.Timestamp{}}}.

It's a huge improvements but it also produce incompatibility issues for clients without this update.
