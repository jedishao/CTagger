Update commit guidelines to not mention contributors and reviewers
The community [decided|https://lists.apache.org/thread/p570y2020jzq5zym1kqvlx22hcddo435] to stop mentioning contributor and reviewer names in the commit message.

The [How To Commit|https://hive.apache.org/docs/latest/howtocommit_27362108/] guidelines should be updated to reflect this decision.
