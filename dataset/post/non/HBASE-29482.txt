This happens because ViewFileSystem cannot generate delegation tokens properly for federated HDFS accessed through ViewFS paths.

Without the delegation token, authentication fails when [checking READ_WRITE permissions on the path where HFiles are stored|https://github.com/apache/hbase/blob/a699d3032887cec9c39868dd93aa7ba84a9b8e03/hbase-server/src/main/java/org/apache/hadoop/hbase/regionserver/HStore.java#L619].

The simplest solution is to use the resolved HDFS paths instead of ViewFS paths when performing the bulkload.
