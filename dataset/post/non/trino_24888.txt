Iceberg Table corruption on spot machines
Hello,

we are running trino 444 on kubernetes gke with the coordinator node on on-demand machines and workers on spot machines.
Fault tolerant execution is enabled and Graceful shutdown procedure is also working when gke kill a spot worker (even if graceful period on gke is only 30 sec).

We write iceberg tables with Hive metastore catalog and we are frequently having table corruptions with errors ICEBERG_MISSING_METADATA
or failed to opening split, missing parquet file.

During a table corruption i was able to see that the time matched with a spot worker being killed by gke
and in the metastore we have an update for the table manifest file, but the new manifest is not on gcs bucket.

Looks like the pointer to the manifest in hive is switched even if the write on gcs is not successfully completed

Unfortunately i do not have usefull logs.

Any idea that can help us troubleshoot this one?

Thanks a lot!
