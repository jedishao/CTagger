add setcacheperiod to nodetool
When I want to restart a Cassandra node, I aim to immediately flush my entire local cache to expedite the cache warm-up process. Currently, I can achieve this by using the _*{{setcacheperiod}}*_ command:
{code:java}
./nodetool setcacheperiod 60 60 60 {code}
This command helps me flush the cache as soon as possible.
