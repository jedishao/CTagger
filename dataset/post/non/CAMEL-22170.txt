Improve performance of loading of routes
One of the most common task in Camel is routing of routes or routeTemplated (templatedRoutes).

It's not slow, but sometimes loading a few routes can take a bit of time (a couple of hundred milliseconds per route) using the routesLoader:


{code:java}
RoutesLoader loader = PluginHelper.getRoutesLoader(context);
loader.updateRoutes(routeResource);{code}

As discussed with [~orpiske] there may be ways to improve which would hugely benefit my setup, but would be beneficial for all Camel users. This ticket is created as a reminder to improve the loading when there is time.
