camel-jbang - version list --fresh should persist the result
After {{camel versoin list --fresh}}, the list of versions should be refreshed locally. Currently, {{camel version list}} always returns a staled list.

{code}
$ camel version list
CAMEL VERSION   JDK   KIND     RELEASED     SUPPORTED UNTIL
    [...]
    4.10.0      17,21  LTS    February 2025    February 2026
    4.10.1      17,21  LTS       March 2025    February 2026
    4.10.2      17,21  LTS       March 2025    February 2026
{code}

{code}
$ camel version list --fresh
CAMEL VERSION   JDK   KIND     RELEASED     SUPPORTED UNTIL
    [...]
    4.10.0       17,21  LTS    February 2025    February 2026
    4.10.1       17,21  LTS       March 2025    February 2026
    4.10.2       17,21  LTS       March 2025    February 2026
    4.10.3      17, 21  LTS       March 2025    February 2026
    4.10.4       17,21  LTS       April 2025    February 2026
    4.11.0      17, 21            April 2025
{code}

and again:

{code}
$ camel version list
CAMEL VERSION   JDK   KIND     RELEASED     SUPPORTED UNTIL
    [...]
    4.10.0      17,21  LTS    February 2025    February 2026
    4.10.1      17,21  LTS       March 2025    February 2026
    4.10.2      17,21  LTS       March 2025    February 2026
{code}
