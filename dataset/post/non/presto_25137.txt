Support partitioned Iceberg DeleteFile
Similar to DataFile, Iceberg DeleteFile can also be partitioned:
DeleteFile
└── extends ContentFile<StructLike>
        ├── filePath()
        ├── partition()
        ├── specId()
        ├── recordCount()
        ├── fileSizeInBytes()
        ├── ...

However `com.facebook.presto.iceberg.delete.DeleteFile` doesn't have the partition related information. Although we haven't seen such cases happening, it can happen in theory. We will need to support it in the future.
