Missing iterator() implementation in AbstractHttp2Headers
Hi,

This looks like a different flavor of an earlier [issue](https://github.com/grpc/grpc-java/pull/10663). The iterator method used [here](https://github.com/netty/netty/blame/4.1/codec-http2/src/main/java/io/netty/handler/codec/http2/DefaultHttp2ConnectionDecoder.java#L425) seems to be unimplemented [here](https://github.com/grpc/grpc-java/blob/master/netty/src/main/java/io/grpc/netty/AbstractHttp2Headers.java#L499)

Is it possible to provide a fix or workaround for this? 

### What version of gRPC-Java are you using?
Using grpc-netty-1.30.2 with Netty 4.1.104.Final. 

### What is your environment?
Linux

### What did you expect to see?
No failures on upgrade from Netty 4.1.100.Final 

### What did you see instead?

Crash with stack trace as below
