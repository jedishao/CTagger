Optimize initial skipping logic for SAI queries on large partitions
In {{StorageAttachedIndexSearcher.ResultRetriever}}, we skip all postings lists produced by queries on individual column indexes to the first partition in the provided {{DataRanges}}. This is an obvious and useful step, but for queries on large partitions, it might not go as far as it could. If paging or user restrictions on the clustering key space are in play, it is probably suboptimal to not even attempt to skip the postings, which are row-addressable, to the first row after the clustering prefix in the (likely single) {{DataRange}}.
