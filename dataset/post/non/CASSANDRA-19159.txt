Add replication to rejection and progress barrier pings
In some cases, we can reduce a number of round-trips in p2p communication. For example, progress barrier can not only attempt to collect majorities, but also send the latest epoch. In majority of cases, node will have to blockingly apply the epoch and progress barrier will succeed right away, resulting into quicker convergence, and we will only have to fill the gaps in case there are any.


During rejection, we also can preventively send the epoch we are at, since the remote processor will attempt to catch up before it makes a retry decision.


Both cases can significantly reduce a number of round trips for simulator, but in prod clusters will probably only be a “nice to have” optimisation.
