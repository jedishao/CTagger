ResultSet may lead to resource leak in DerbyConnector
`org.apache.druid.metadata.storage.derby.DerbyConnector#tableHasColumn`  ResultSet columns

![Image](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/ae558660-59b1-45e8-819d-7777fdd2ff21)

`ResultSet` implements the java.lang.AutoCloseable interface. This means it is a resource that holds onto database resources
It seems that `ResultSet` is never closed,  which may lead to resource leak. I suggest to use a `try-with-resources block`, which ensures the ResultSet is automatically closed.
