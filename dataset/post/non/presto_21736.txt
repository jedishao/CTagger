[documentation] improve CONTRIBUTING.md with a table of ways to contribute and first steps for each
The Presto [CONTRIBUTING.md](https://github.com/prestodb/presto/blob/master/CONTRIBUTING.md) page could benefit from content similar to the "What do you want to do?" table at the beginning of the [Contributing page for Apache Paimon](https://paimon.apache.org/docs/master/project/contributing/) page: 

<img width="902" alt="Screenshot 2024-01-19 at 3 09 04 PM" src="https://github.com/prestodb/presto/assets/7013443/30c5a1c8-a9ef-4187-9aa4-95c014dc2ce5">

Adding similar content to [CONTRIBUTING.md](https://github.com/prestodb/presto/blob/master/CONTRIBUTING.md) could make it easier and faster for people new to Presto to contribute in the way they immediately want to, by directing them based on their intended contribution. 

## Expected Behavior or Use Case
Users who want to contribute to Presto can find how to do so quickly and easily. 

## Presto Component, Service, or Connector
Documentation. 

## Possible Implementation
Update [CONTRIBUTING.md](https://github.com/prestodb/presto/blob/master/CONTRIBUTING.md) with content inspired by the example in the What do you want to do? section of the [Contributing page for Apache Paimon](https://paimon.apache.org/docs/master/project/contributing/).
