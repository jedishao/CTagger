Multiple locking of RLock reset remainTimeToLive to config default
**Actual behavior**

My use case is the following:
```java
RedissonClient redisson = Redisson.create(config);
RLock lock = redisson.getLock("test-lock:1");
lock.lock(10, TimeUnit.SECONDS);
lock.lock(10, TimeUnit.SECONDS);
lock.unlock();
System.out.println(lock.remainTimeToLive()); // prints something close to the config default of 30 000
```

**Expected behavior**

I am not exactly sure but the behaviour changed between 3.19.1 and 3.19.2 most certainly because of https://github.com/redisson/redisson/issues/4818

I would expect that remainTimeToLive is closer to 10 seconds.

**Redis version**

Version 7

**Redisson version**

3.19.2 to 3.26.0
