Future Chain Racing Condition
### Version

Vert.x 4.2.6, Vert.x 4+

### Context

Encountered some sort of racing condition between lambdas in the Future composition chain when certain Operation(s) are ordered in certain way and when initial future comes from executeBlocking().

### Steps to reproduce

Reproducer attached as racing-reproducer.zip gradle project.
Read comments in src/main/java/reproducer/ProblemReproducer.java

1. Unzip racing-reproducer.zip
2. run: ./gradlew reproducer
3. Look for "racing failures;" in program output

JVM version: 17 and 11
OS version: Windows 11 and Windows 10
[racing-reproducer.zip](https://github.com/eclipse-vertx/vert.x/files/8456648/racing-reproducer.zip)
