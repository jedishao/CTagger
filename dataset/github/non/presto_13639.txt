GeometryToBingTiles fails on certain degenerate geometries
It fails on geometries whose max longitude is -180 (or min latitude is MAX_LAT).  Example geom is `'LINESTRING (-180 -79.19245, -180 -79.17133464081945)'`.  It dies in getTileCoveringLowerRightCorner, because it tries to construct a bing tile with x=-1.
