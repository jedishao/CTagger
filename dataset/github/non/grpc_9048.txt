rls: AdaptiveThrottler should use Ticker, not TimeProvider
Ticker is powered by System.nanoTime() which is CLOCK_MONOTONIC.
TimeProvider is powered by System.currentTimeMillis() which is
CLOCK_REALTIME. For durations, the monotonic clock is appropriate, not
the wall time which can jump around.

Care needs to be taken when converting because Ticker [time is allowed to
overflow](https://docs.oracle.com/javase/8/docs/api/java/lang/System.html#nanoTime--).
TimeBasedAccumulator looked a bit too involved for me to convert without
looking at it more closely.
