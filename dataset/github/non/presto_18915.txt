Pushdown partial agg below JOIN when the join keys match group by keys
We have seen queries like:
```
SELECT agg... FROM T1 JOIN T2 USING(k) JOIN T3 USING(k) GROU BY k
```
This can actually do at least partial agg (or maybe even the full agg?) before join. Will be a good optimization to add.

We can also make it general where the GROUP BY keys is a subset of the join keys.
