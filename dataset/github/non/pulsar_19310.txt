### Motivation


<img width="960" alt="image" src="https://user-images.githubusercontent.com/15684156/214112188-a174ca40-406d-49b6-9ccc-963a53f3065e.png">

When I upgrade a cluster, I always find that the last broker to be upgraded has no traffic.

I need to unload it manually before traffic comes in.

This can lead to long periods of unbalanced load on the cluster.

### Solution

Automatically unload the broker with the higher load when there are idle nodes in the cluster.
