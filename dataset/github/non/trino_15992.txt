`maven-checks` job fails if Ubuntu repositories go down
We build a Dockerfile which runs `apt` as part of our CI pipeline.
This introduces a dependency on external Ubuntu repositories, and could fail CI with no fault of our own.

Examples:
- https://github.com/trinodb/trino/actions/runs/4004028582/jobs/6872683430 - https://github.com/trinodb/trino/pull/15807#issuecomment-1403381201
