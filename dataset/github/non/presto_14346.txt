Presto connection to kerberized Hive and HDFS on CentOS 8
When we try to connect the presto to kerberized Hive and HDFS on CentOS 8 it gives below error. Probably issue looks to be Kerberos encryption issue, but not sure. However, the same thing runs perfectly fine on CentOS 7.

To enable Kerberos, debug logging.. chang the etc/jvm.config file, 

-server
-Xmx16G
-XX:+UseG1GC
-XX:G1HeapRegionSize=32M
-XX:+UseGCOverheadLimit
-XX:+ExplicitGCInvokesConcurrent
-XX:+HeapDumpOnOutOfMemoryError
-XX:+ExitOnOutOfMemoryError
-Dsun.security.krb5.debug=true
