optimized DATE parsing and formatting by caching recent dates
date parsing and formatting functions are widely used, and is expensive. After studying to our daily queries, most of them are parsing or formatting the recent dates, that means most of the function calls are dealing with the same input, which is not necessary, and can be improved with a cache.

so I'd like to proposal a PR to address this issue by cacheing the recent (like say last 10 years) date in a map.

the above mentioned functions are as below:
date(‘2022-10-14’)
date_parse(dt, '%Y-%m-%d')
cast(‘2022-10-14’ as date)
date_format(dt,'%Y-%m-%d')
format_datetime(dt, 'yyyy-MM-dd')
