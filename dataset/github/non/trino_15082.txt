possibly erroneous "Heap Memory" information in `ui/worker.html?{nodeId}`
I have a cluster (version 380) with JVM config as `-Xmx286637m -Xms286637m` and in the UI for a worker (`ui/worker.html?{nodeId}`) I am seeing a different value:

![Screenshot 2022-11-17 at 11 26 44](https://user-images.githubusercontent.com/1939193/202472732-bf1edcd4-8419-43c0-a87c-22c7a7025714.png)

`Xmx` and `Xms` are power of 1024 as 1g is equal to 1024m. 286637 MiB is ~ 300 GiB. However the UI for a worker shows 280GB.

It might be some conversion issue (maybe related to [formatDataSize](https://github.com/trinodb/trino/blob/189da556eae0ef77f1f66d96b2ae3d209a6fe8ce/core/trino-main/src/main/resources/webapp/src/components/WorkerStatus.jsx#L277) in front-end) since 300 GB is 280 GiB or JVM allocates some other limit according to given `Xmx` value.
