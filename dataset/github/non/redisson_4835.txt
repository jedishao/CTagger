**Is your feature request related to a problem? Please describe.**
Not really 


**Describe the solution you'd like**
Currently `RMapCache` `putAll` only support:
`void putAll(java.util.Map<? extends K, ? extends V> map, long ttl, TimeUnit ttlUnit);`
There is no way to update the maxIdleTime at the putAll method

Support for `RMapCache putAll(map, ttl, ttlUnit, maxIdleTime, maxIdleUnit)`


**Describe alternatives you've considered**
Currently, we loop it and use the single put to update the idle time
