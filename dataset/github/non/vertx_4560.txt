Disable TLS 1.0 and 1.1 protocols by default
Vert.x enables by default the use of TLS 1.0 and 1.1 in addition of other protocols (>= 1.2), such protocols are considered deprecated and the JDK since April 2021 no longer enables them by default.

Vert.x should also disable them by default. This does not prevent their use through proper configuration of the TCP SSL options configuration.
