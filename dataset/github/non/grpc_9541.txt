NameResolver should explicitly state that methods executed in SynchronizationContext must not throw
When a `NameResolver` implementation throws from `start(Listener)`, it leads to a panic in `ManagedChannelImpl` because of unhandled exception in `SynchronizationContext`.  After that, the channel becomes unusable.

The suggestion is to make the no-throw contract for `NameResolver.start()` explicit.
