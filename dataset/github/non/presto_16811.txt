[Feature Request] Add two-sample Kolmogorov–Smirnov & Anderson-Darling tests
The two-sample KS and AD tests compare distributions ([info](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kolmogorov%E2%80%93Smirnov_test#Two-sample_Kolmogorov%E2%80%93Smirnov_test)).  They also have the nice property that it's easy to estimate them given TDigests or QDigests of the two distributions.  

Would these be good candidates to add to Presto itself or is this better-scoped to a UDF?  Together with two coworkers, I'm interested in implementing this in either case.
