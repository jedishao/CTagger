### What issue do you find in Pulsar docs?

The following information excerpts from the previous discussion with @asafm and @jak78 .

This [Docker deployment doc](https://pulsar.apache.org/docs/next/deploy-docker/) page has two sections: 
* docker commands - serve as a reference for docker commands, for people who are composing their own scripts.
* docker-compose - quickly set up a cluster on a local laptop for DevOps.

Generally, users won't use `docker-compose` for deployment, but for quick start instead - get Pulsar up and ready in a laptop / dev-env. So `docker-compose` suits the motivation of getting started more - allow developers and dev-ops to "test drive" Pulsar in the comfort and speed of their laptops.
