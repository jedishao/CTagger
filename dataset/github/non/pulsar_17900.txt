What is the proposed workflow?
- for most of these pages it should start with an explanation with a link to create a PR or Issue at the bottom of the page.
- for the team / community the PMC as whole should be responsible. Members of the PMC are PMC Members and not **PMCs**. PMCs are ambiguous, it could mean a group of people or more than one project management committee. The format of the Meet the Community section is another issue.
