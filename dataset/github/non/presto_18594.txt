Decorrelate subqueries containing OrderBy + Limit or Limit Clause
Presto throws this error: `Correlated sub-query is not supported` for correlated subqueries that contain `OrderBy and Limit` or just `Limit` clauses. 

Here's a sample query that caused this exception to the raised:
`SELECT "tbl1"."id" FROM "A" "tbl1" WHERE (SELECT "tbl2"."id" FROM "B" "tbl2" WHERE "tbl2"."x"="tbl1"."y" ORDER BY "tbl2"."z" DESC LIMIT 1) = 'randomid'`

Trino made some optimizations to decorrelate such quries. Here's the PR: https://github.com/trinodb/trino/pull/1415

Could we add support for decorrelating subqueries containing OrderBy + Limit or just the Limit clauses?
