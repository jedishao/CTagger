Error : Building/launching Presto with OpenJDK 16 and 17
OpenJDK 16 and 17 introduces several new features,  and it’d be beneficial to have those features available in Presto.
Currently, building/launching Presto with OpenJDK 16 and 17 causes some errors. Observed errors and proposed solutions are documented below. The errors were observed when building with -DskipTest (tests still need to be examined).
