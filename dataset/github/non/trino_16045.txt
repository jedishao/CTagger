Trino at scale: health checking, monitoring, and observability
As we scale Trino larger and larger, multiple clusters, we need near real-time health checking, monitoring and observability of Trino. Not just Trino but even the health/connectivity of the underlying catalog system.

I am wondering if other Trino users are running into these needs? Here is a starting list of our requirements. I am wondering if others have addressed these issue.

- Query stats
- User stats CPU/Memory
- Catalog health check
- Kubernetes health
- SQL errors
- Trino crash stacks
