Remove redundant protobuf exclusions
When [bumping Iceberg from 1.0.0 to 1.1.0](https://github.com/trinodb/trino/pull/15079), we transitively bumped ORC from 1.7.x to 1.8.x. With 1.8.x comes protobuf files that shouldn't be packaged. These will be removed in ORC 1.8.2: https://issues.apache.org/jira/browse/ORC-1327 ([PR](https://github.com/apache/orc/pull/1334)).
