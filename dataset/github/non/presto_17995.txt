SqlFormatter does not handle EnumLiterals
`ExpressionFormatter` and subsequently `SqlFormatter` currently do not handle enum literals when formatting an expression (see [`ExpressionFormatter`](https://github.com/prestodb/presto/blob/master/presto-parser/src/main/java/com/facebook/presto/sql/ExpressionFormatter.java) and [`SqlFormatter`](https://github.com/prestodb/presto/blob/master/presto-parser/src/main/java/com/facebook/presto/sql/SqlFormatter.java)).
