Delta `CommitInfoEntry`'s `operationParameters` has the incorrect type
Reported by Sujith Kumar.S on [Slack](https://trinodb.slack.com/archives/C03E2DYAS0J/p1675609218304289)

This field https://github.com/trinodb/trino/blob/master/plugin/trino-delta-lake/src/main/java/io/trino/plugin/deltalake/transactionlog/CommitInfoEntry.java#L31 should be a `Map<String, Object>`, not `Map<String, String>`

It's not spelled out well in the specification, but it has an example with an array value: https://github.com/delta-io/delta/blob/master/PROTOCOL.md#commit-provenance-information
