by reading the source code, found out that since a while ago, Druid requires shardSpec to be consistent inside a data source to do ingestion. 
our ingestion path is from Kafka, therefore the shardSpec is "numbered". 
and we also do overnight reindexing, which shardSpec can only choose from either "hashed" or "single-dim".
therefore no matter what the shardSpec will be different. and then ingestion will break like above. 

any idea to workaround this ?
