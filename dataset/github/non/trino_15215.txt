Push dictionary through remote partitioned exchange
Dictionary-encoded blocks are currently flattened by the partitioned exchange operator. This prevents dictionary-based optimizations from taking advantage of the encoded blocks (or results in additional overhead).

https://github.com/trinodb/trino/pull/14937 adds support for pushing dictionary blocks through the remote exchange but benchmarks show regressions for some queries when the dictionary blocks are pushed through.

There are two main reasons for regressions.
1. (more minor impact) `PartitioningExchanger` (i.e. local partitioned exchange) uses `copyPositions` to distribute positions to partitions which is not free for `DictionaryBlocks`
2. it makes the join build side operate on 3 types/classes of Blocks (flat, rle, dictionary) inside the `PagesHashStrategy`. This causes a big performance drop (> 2x in some cases) in `HashBuilderOperator` due to virtual call overhead. It can also impact the join probe side or `HashAggregationOperator` but I haven't seen that confirmed by a real query.
