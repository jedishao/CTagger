Future terminal operations clarification
In some parts of the stack, it is assumed that `Future` terminal operations (`onSuccess`, `onFailure`, `onComplete`) are executed in order of registration.

While in most cases this is true, it is not guaranteed and can lead to bugs like https://github.com/vert-x3/vertx-jdbc-client/issues/281

The actual behavior should be clarified in documentation and we should inspect the stack to find possible problems.
