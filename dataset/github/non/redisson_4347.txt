How the "nodeAddresses" differentiate master/slave in the clusterServersConfig mode?
Hi Redisson community,

I think I have a dumb question but I didn't find anywhere explain it. Hope anyone can give me a hint.

Let's assume we have a 6 nodes Redis cluster (3 masters and 3 slaves). And we have 2 different configurations for Redisson client.

**Version A**

```
clusterServersConfig:
  ...
  ...
  ...
  slaveConnectionMinimumIdleSize: 100
  slaveConnectionPoolSize: 300
  masterConnectionMinimumIdleSize: 100
  masterConnectionPoolSize: 300
  readMode: "SLAVE"
  ...
  nodeAddresses:
  - "redis://master1.com:6379"
  - "redis://master2.com:6379"
  - "redis://master3.com:6379"
  - "redis://slave1.con:6380"
  - "redis://slave2.con:6380"
  - "redis://slave3.con:6380"
```

**Version B**

```
clusterServersConfig:
  ...
  ...
  ...
  slaveConnectionMinimumIdleSize: 100
  slaveConnectionPoolSize: 300
  masterConnectionMinimumIdleSize: 100
  masterConnectionPoolSize: 300
  readMode: "MASTER_SLAVE"
  ...
  nodeAddresses:
  - "redis://master1.com:6379"
  - "redis://master2.com:6379"
  - "redis://master3.com:6379"
```


I test of these 2 different types of configurations in my environment, and I don't notice anything different. But I wonder 

1. In the "Version A", I put all master & slave together in the "nodeAddresses". Is Redisson client able to differentiate the master and slave? And then only route the "READ" operation to slave nodes?
2. In the "Version B", I put "readMode: MASTER_SLAVE", but I only gave master nodes addresses in "nodeAddresses". Will Redisson client find the slave nodes and balance the "READ" operation to slave nodes?
