Documentation omits several Metastore and Thrift Configuration Properties ?
The Hive metastore can be configured with a number of properties, like `hive.metastore-cache-ttl` and `hive.metastore-refresh-interval` - a good deal of these seem to be missing from the documentation. 

Other omissions may or may not include `hive.metastore-cache-maximum-size` and `hive.metastore-refresh-max-threads` and some thrift related properties.
