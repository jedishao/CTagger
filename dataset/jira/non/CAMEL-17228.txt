camel-kamelet - Kamelet EIP restarting issue
If you use kamelet eip in Camel routes and you want to stop and remove this route, then there can be a problem with shutting down graceful.
Also it may be a problem if you then add a route afterwards that use kamelet eip as well.
eg if you use route-reload (such as karavan with jbang)
https://github.com/apache/camel-karavan/issues/112
