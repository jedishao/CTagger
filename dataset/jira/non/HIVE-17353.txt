The ResultSets are not accessible if running multiple queries within the same HiveStatement 
The following queries would fail,
{noformat}
    ResultSet rs1 =
        stmt.executeQuery("select * from testMultipleResultSets1");
    ResultSet rs2 =
        stmt.executeQuery("select * from testMultipleResultSets2");
    rs1.next();
    rs2.next();
{noformat}
