Migrate the thresholds for RFP cached rows to guardrails
Migrate the existing thresholds for replica filtering protection:


replica_filtering_protection:
    cached_rows_warn_threshold: 2000
    cached_rows_fail_threshold: 32000


To a new guardrail under the guardrails section, for example:


guardrails:
    ...
    replica_filtering_protection_cached_rows:
        warn_threshold: 2000
        abort_threshold: 32000


At the moment these thresholds are enabled by default, while guardrails are globally disabled by default, so we should consider whether we want to globally enable guardrails, or change these thresholds to disabled by default, or don't migrate the thresholds.
