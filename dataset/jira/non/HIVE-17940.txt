(This is a backport of HIVE-10024 to {{branch-1.2}}, and {{branch-1}}.)

[~sershe] had a fix for this in HIVE-10024, in {{branch-2}}. After running into this in production with {{branch-1}}+, we find that the fix for HIVE-10024 sorts this out in {{branch-1}} as well.
