camel-core - Supervise route controller to supervise all the time
Today the supervise route controller is only active during startup to "fix" the problem with starting up routes and to retry starting routes if they failed during startup. When all is started then the supervised route controller will terminate itself.
The controller should play a bigger role by

act as single gateway into the health check, management, and monitoring of the routes
so it should keep monitoring the routes
act as health check facade for all the routes
have configurable strategies for users to specify criteria's for when a route is UP or DOWN (eg DOWN after 5 consecutive errors, and so on)
be able to stop routes in case they start to fail
be able to start routes again after X period to see if they can become succesfull again
and so on...
