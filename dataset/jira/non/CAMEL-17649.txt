Examples for setHeader in example are outdated: it contains name attribute instead of headerName
see https://camel.apache.org/components/3.15.x/eips/setHeader-eip.html#_using_set_header

<route>
    <from uri="direct:a"/>
    <setHeader name="myHeader">
        <constant>test</constant>
    </setHeader>
    <to uri="direct:b"/>
</route>


I think that it should be:

<route>
    <from uri="direct:a"/>
    <setHeader headerName="myHeader">
        <constant>test</constant>
    </setHeader>
    <to uri="direct:b"/>
</route>


it was renamed with 3.0 https://camel.apache.org/manual/camel-3-migration-guide.html#_setheader_and_setproperty_in_xml_dsl
