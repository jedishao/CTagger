Does logback delayed shutdown hook do anything?
It seems like the delayed shutdown hook from logback is used for both production (programmatically in StorageService) and declaratively in the configuration of logback for tests.
Though, the default delay is zero, and we do not configure the delay anywhere - so it because both useless and actually wrong if we expect a time for appenders flush the outputs (or I missed something).
I propose a different approach - a shutdown hook which would just go through all the appenders and first enable immediate flushing, and then flush the output streams. This way we would not have to wait and we will be sure that everything is properly flushed. 
Does that make sense?
