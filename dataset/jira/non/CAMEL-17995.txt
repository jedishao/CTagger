REST DSL YAML Generator should optionally generate routes
In addition to REST DSL, ex:


- rest:
    put:
      path: "/pet"
      to: "direct:rest1"


generator should optionally generate routes, ex:


- rest:
    put:
      path: "/pet"
      to: "direct:rest1"
- route:
    from:
      uri: "direct:rest1"
