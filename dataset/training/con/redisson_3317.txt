```
        RLock rLock = redisson.getLock(mapName + "@" + obj.getKey());

        redisson.getMap(mapName).put(obj.getKey(), obj.getValue());
```

Why is it so? why do i need to trigger rLock.unlock(); multiple times until it finally removes lock?
