[Broker] Mutations of propertiesMap is not threadsafe in ManagedLedgerImpl
### Problem

`propertiesMap` is mutated and read in multiple threads, it's backed by an ordinary HashMap.
There is a comment in the code related to thread safety in `org.apache.bookkeeper.mledger.impl.ManagedLedgerImpl`:

https://github.com/apache/pulsar/blob/1b8da9245e111718acb337bce310ffdef7b30496/managed-ledger/src/main/java/org/apache/bookkeeper/mledger/impl/ManagedLedgerImpl.java#L187-L190

However, it seems that this type of solution doesn't prevent thread safety issues when multiple threads are involved and there are mutations happening in multiple threads. 

Unsafe mutations and access of HashMaps is a critical problem. It can cause data corruption and threads might go into infinite loops.
