ThriftHiveMetastore#getPartitionStatistics is leaking getPartitionsByNames calls within transaction cache
`ThriftHiveMetastore#getPartitionStatistics` calls `getPartitionsByNames` underneath in order to obtain partition stats. However, partitions are normally cached by `CachingHiveMetastore` per transaction. This doubles Metastore partition queries when number of partitions is below 100. The overhead might be significant when there are many concurrent queries (e.g. 20).
