Bad connector can hang worker
If a connector hangs in a call to RecordSet, all threads in the worker can become deadlocked:

```
   TaskExecutorThread: 
       PrioritizedSplitRunner.process()
       DriverSplitRunner.processFor()
       Driver.process() <lock>
       Hang due to bug in connector

   CallbackThread: 
       StateMachine<Task> transition to a done state
       TaskExecutor.removeTask() <lock>
       DriverSplitRunner.close() <lock> [deadlock]
       Driver.close() <lock> (next call so we would deadlock here also)

   AllHttpWorkerThreads: 
       TaskResource.createOrUpdateTask
       SqlTaskManager.updateTask <lock>
       TaskExecutor.addTask <lock>
```
