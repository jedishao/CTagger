`tryLock` in redis cluster working not properly
<!--

-->

**Expected behavior**
`tryLock` of RLock can not return `true` when the lock key still exist in cluster redis.

**Actual behavior**
Sometimes `tryLock` can acquire the lock key although it still exists. Moreover, the TTL of the lock key also is reset. 

**Steps to reproduce or test case**
Invoke `boolean res = lock.tryLock(100, 3600, TimeUnit.SECONDS)` => res = true. Then after 10s, TTL of the key is 3590, in another thread, invoke `boolean res = lock.tryLock(100, 3600, TimeUnit.SECONDS)` => res = true. TTL of the key is 3600

**Redis version**
5.0.7
**Redisson version**
3.12.5
