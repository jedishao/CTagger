Multiple injectors can cause racy or locking behavior in GuiceFilter
If a task tries to create the startup injector at the same time while the main creator is being instantiated, AND both are in the same classloader, then there is some racy handling in GuiceFilter that can cause deadlocks in the initialization process. Here are the thread dumps of some locked threads as an example:
