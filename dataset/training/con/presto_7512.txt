Looking at the code of `org.apache.bval.jsr.ConstraintValidation`, the class is _terribly not thread safe_ and lazy initializes `validator` field. Hence `MinDataSizeValidator.isValid(MinDataSizeValidator.java:33)` can fail with NPE because `isValid` is called "before" `initialize`.
