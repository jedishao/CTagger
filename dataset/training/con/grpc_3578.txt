Stress test client hangs
With the inclusion of `io.opencensus:opencensus-impl:0.7.0` as a runtime dependency (added to our interop test clients in https://github.com/grpc/grpc-java/commit/8edead08510ccd382087f114fb4833b8170698d5#diff-dbbdfba18c8958c51b031ef112a6fa7cR29), our stress test client hangs.

## Steps to reproduce:

### Run interop server:

`build/install/grpc-interop-testing/bin/test-server`

### Run stress client:

`build/install/grpc-interop-testing/bin/stresstest-client --server_addresses=localhost:8080 --server_host_override=foo.test.google.fr --use_tls=true --use_test_ca=true --test_cases=empty_unary:100 --metrics_port=0 --test_duration_secs=1 --num_channels_per_server=1 --num_stubs_per_channel=1`

The client will finish its 1 second of testing and successfully terminate the channel, its metrics server, and its own threadpool, but the client process does not terminate; it just hangs. jstack output includes the following:
