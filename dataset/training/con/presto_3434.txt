Queries with complex and many stages result in "unable to create new native thread" 
It looks like that there's too much of new threads spawned instantly once a very complex query is started. It feels to me that Presto shouldn't crash under whatever query, so we can make it reliable and robust to whatever someone inputs. 

The cluster become unresponsive after such a query, with new queries hanging in QUEUED state. We haven't seen it recover from this automatically.

Possible solution would be to limit the amount of threads, which currently doesn't seem the case.
