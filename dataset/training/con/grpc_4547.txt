ClientCall#cancel() implementations should be thread-safe
Calling ```ListenableFuture.cancel(true)``` on the stub returned by ```ServiceNameGrpc.newFutureStub(Channel channel)``` calls through directly to the underlying ClientCall#cancel() method.  ListenableFuture#cancel() may be invoked from any thread.

ClientCall implementations (and in particular [ClientCallImpl](https://github.com/grpc/grpc-java/blob/master/core/src/main/java/io/grpc/internal/ClientCallImpl.java)) should be fixed safely handle this, and the ClientCall docs updated.

See PR  #4539 for additional context.
