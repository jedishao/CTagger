Support complex condition on synthetic $path column
We want to achieve an effect similar to bucketing in a hive table by predicate push down on the synthetic $path column. Right now, this only works for equality clause or IN clause, but does not work when a complex condition is used, such as "$path" LIKE '%xyz%' .
Per discussion on the slack thread  https://prestosql.slack.com/archives/CFLB9AMBN/p1578312918226100 
It seems that it might be possible to intervene between the directory listing of a hive partition, and before split generation
