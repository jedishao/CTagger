LIKE predicate not supported as part of join clause
Only queries with comparison operators in JOIN clause are allowed now in Presto.

E.g. query like:

```
select customer.custkey ck, orders.orderkey ok from customer join orders on customer.custkey = orders.custkey and orders.comment like 'ala%';
```

willl fail with `Non-equi joins not supported: ("orders"."comment" LIKE 'ala%')` error.
