Jackson version conflict with avatica
There is a conflict with jackson-databind 2.6.3 (from transitive dependencies of calcite-core and avatica-core) and druid's explicit jackson-databind 2.4.6 dependency. I see issues during batch ingestion. Realtime using tranquility is fine. Here is a gist containing the stack trace

Druid has direct dependency on `calcite-core`, `avatica-core` and `avatica-server` from https://github.com/druid-io/druid/pull/3682.

`avatica-core` is the non-shaded artifact which only contains client classes without any bundled dependencies except jackson. as per this: https://github.com/apache/calcite/commit/e5a42f1ca80eca0bb6f0c8f6677f106fadb2e652#diff-eb4d33b91f63eeaaea2ffd36688b61f8R49

`calcite-core` depends on shaded avatica.
`avatica-server` depends on non-shaded avatica
`avatica-core` is the non-shaded avatica.
