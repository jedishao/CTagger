Large change in cache hit rate for 0.9.1
We are seeing a significant change in cache hit rate and response rate for 0.9.1 vs 0.9.0. 

![screen shot 2016-06-20 at 2 35 23 pm](https://cloud.githubusercontent.com/assets/8213081/16213815/2ce9c74c-3707-11e6-8193-62be29db0154.png)

The above screenshot shows cache hit rate over time. Vertical lines are day boundaries. The discontinuity and drop in cache hit rate directly correlates with the 0.9.1 rollout to this node. Rolling back to 0.9.0 causes an increase in reported cache hit rate.

The Memcached metric  `[MEM] Response Rate: All (Failure + Success + Retry)` correlates with the change in cache hit rate. 

The only queries registered on this node are of type dataSourceMetadata, topN, or timeseries.

I don't have enough data yet to determine if there is an impact on query performance.
