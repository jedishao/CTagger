After some investigation, I [found](https://github.com/druid-io/druid/pull/2223/files#r154703751) out that the real config is ignored, and what hadoop gets instead is this:

```java
JobConf dummyConf = new JobConf();
```

 In the PR that it was introduced in, the only discussion of this line is that `dummyConf` should be a local variable instead of a field: https://github.com/druid-io/druid/pull/2223#discussion_r49161015

I am currently looking for a workaround, but this should be fixed for good. However, l cannot understand how the `dummyConf` was supposed to ever work. Maybe @navis can help explain?
