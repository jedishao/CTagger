coordinator auto merge segment can cause dirty data
My scenario as follows:
1. druid version is  `0.9.2`.
2. `enable` coordinator auto merge.
3. coordinator  merge data from `2018-01-01` to `2018-05-01`.
4.  if i reindex the data between `2018-01-01` to `2018-03-01` from HDFS.
5.  then we will find two segment  which has `2018-01-01/2018-05-01` and  `2018-01-01/2018-03-01` in datasource. 
6.  if i reindex the data between `2018-03-01` to `2018-05-01` from HDFS.
7.  then there is only  `2018-01-01/2018-05-01`  in datasource and no dirty data.
