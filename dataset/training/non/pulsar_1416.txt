Docs about garbage collection are inaccurate/lacking
In the docs (http://pulsar.apache.org/docs/latest/admin/GeoReplication/#creating-global-namespaces) the section "Delete a global topic" says:

> In Pulsar, a topic is automatically deleted when it’s no longer used, that is to say, when no producers or consumers are connected and there are no subscriptions

I believe it should say that it will also not be garbage collected if there are messages with retention > 0. And could be worth mentioning that you can turn this off with brokerDeleteInactiveTopicsEnabled=false.
