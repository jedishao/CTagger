[Client] Donate Haskell client to Pulsar 
When browsing the Pulsar website, I found that @gvolpe has developed a wonderful [Haskell client for Pulsar](https://github.com/cr-org/supernova) and draw much attention. 

Since good projects are collaborations between motivated individuals, to attract more programmers to share quality code, appeal to more technical writers to develop docs, and to allow more users to use it conveniently, as suggested by @codelipenghui, I asked @gvolpe if he'd like to donate the Haskell client to Pulsar, and the answer is yes.

Here is @gvolpe's reply:

> Sure, I'd be happy to donate it over to the Apache organization. The client is still in early stage but the basic workflow of consumers and producers works fine. I haven't had much time to work on it ever since.
> 
> Feel free to reach out directly to hello@gvolpe.com to let me know how we should proceed and what the implications might be (if any).
