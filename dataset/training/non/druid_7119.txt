[materialized view] The last day of month and the last 24h are not ingested by the Supervisor
Based on what @zhangxinyu1 [said](https://github.com/apache/incubator-druid/issues/6945#issuecomment-459635347):

> The timeline of derivativeDataSource is expected to be the same with baseDataSource's. Your can get the timeline of a dataSource from coordinator UI.

and inspecting the timeline, the derivative data source has one day missing (the last day of the month).
